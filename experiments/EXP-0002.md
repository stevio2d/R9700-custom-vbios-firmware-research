## Environment and procedure

Use the model and service arguments retained in EXP-0001: exact TP4, maximum
context 262144, eight sequences, fp8 cache, MTP depth 3, batching/checkpoints 2048,
expert_tiered, host pool zero, ngram in RAM and GPU headroom 96 MiB. Do not adopt
the newer release's compose defaults or new cache formats.

The [bounded runner](../tools/radiance_version_trial.py) freezes source/corpus
hashes, records full request definitions, corrects decode timing through response
completion, and requires complete responses and finite phase metrics. Each
repetition has fixed but fresh nonce inputs matched across versions.

The [mini-spec](RADIANCE_COMPARISON.md) defines the eight categories, five prompt
depths, four concurrency levels, response probes, evidence and decision rules.
The outer supervisor enforces 28 minutes for one version including container
hashing, probes and all repeats. Cleanup has two further minutes of allowance.
Build time is separate. Failed/incomplete runs do not establish a speedup.

Preparation verification: nine focused host tests passed; three local mock
suites produced 39 recorded requests, with matching request hashes for matching
repetitions and distinct nonces for different repetitions. The required code
review found no remaining major issue. These are harness checks, not GPU results.

## Stop conditions and rollback

Continuously inspect new GPU reset/fault/hang, relevant PCIe timeout/uncorrectable
errors, request failures and each sensor's advertised critical temperature.
Missing required monitoring stops the run. Advertised thresholds are precautionary
stop points, not independently validated exact-board safety limits. Ambient
and fan reporting remain unknown until recorded; do not invent them.

The client stops on a fault without automatically restarting the server. Preserve
the 1.6.0 installation and launch for rollback if 1.9.0 is incompatible, slower,
or fails response checks. No GPU resets, driver reloads or host reboot are
authorized as automatic recovery actions.

## Actual runs and results

The 1.6.0 baseline completed all three repetitions in 1152.25 seconds
(19.20 minutes). All 840 recorded streaming requests passed, including warmups;
the six before/after response texts matched. The model hash matches the expected
container. The filtered kernel error log is empty. The highest recorded GPU
junction temperature was 100 °C, below its advertised 110 °C critical threshold.

The [offline baseline summary](EXP-0002/A-summary.json) retains medians, ranges,
request counts and hashes of the [raw evidence](EXP-0002/A-1.6.0/status.json).
Candidate 1.9.0 built in its separate prefix and started as PID 74995.
All 1051 source-file hashes match the downloaded archive. The build needed a
target-scoped BMI2 compiler flag for its unused AVX-512 tier; see the mini-spec.

The [host test log](EXP-0002/build-1.9.0/host-tests.log) records **81/88 passing**
in 73.91 seconds. Failures: image/video tests require omitted FFmpeg support;
two kernel-benchmark identity/cache tests fail (including absent build IDs);
GBNF shard 2 and tokenizer checks exceed CPU budgets; GBNF shard 3 produces an
invalid Unicode sequence in a JSON-schema walk (seed 69). That correctness
failure prevents automatic release adoption even if throughput improves.
No tolerance or test budget was relaxed. B completed in 1128.37 seconds
(18.81 minutes), below its 1560-second cap; its host checks plus throughput
trial totaled 20.04 minutes, with startup and cleanup also inside 30 minutes.
All 840 recorded B requests passed, and all before/after response probe texts
and token usage matched A. All six repetitions are completed actual runs.

## Interpretation and follow-up

- 1.9.0 improves client-observed prefill by 5.70%, 5.55%, 3.59% and 2.24%
  at target depths 2k, 8k, 16k and 32k. All four exceed the predeclared 2%
  threshold and the observed repeat ranges. The 64k gain is 1.61%, below that
  threshold. Prefill here is prompt tokens divided by client TTFT.
- Weighted decode rises 0.85% (272.50 to 274.81 tok/s), below the promotion
  threshold. Concurrency one improves 2.99%; concurrency two rises 1.72%.
  Concurrency four/eight fall 1.30%/1.36%; their observed ranges overlap A,
  and neither is a demonstrated material regression under the decision rule.
- Throughput evidence favors 1.9.0 for prefill, with little decode benefit.
  It does not override the failed host correctness check. The retained
  [verification record](EXP-0002/VERIFICATION.md) explains why that failure is
  not yet a demonstrated regression from 1.6.0.
- Keep production on 1.6.0 pending diagnosis and baseline selection. Its
  restoration passed all six response probes with matching usage. The
  [restoration record](EXP-0002/restore-1.6.0/restoration.json) verifies identical
  executable/library hashes, arguments and model stat identity under new PID
  83854. The standard launcher PID file was updated; its log continues at the
  original path. Restart throughput
  variability and general quality remain unmeasured.
- No custom dispatch or compute-kernel change was applied during EXP-0002.
  The operator subsequently selected isolated user-space HIP trials against
  1.9.0 in [EXP-0003](EXP-0003.md). The host-test failures still block automatic
  permanent release adoption, and the installed/build Linux-module provenance
  discrepancy remains unqualified. EXP-0003 holds driver and boot identity fixed;
  it includes no Linux-module experiment. The release's source differences are
  retained in [upstream findings](EXP-0002/UPSTREAM_FINDINGS.md).
